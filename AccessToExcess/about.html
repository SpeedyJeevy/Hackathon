Who We Are

Community First

Access to Excess is a community-driven platform that connects local restaurants with shelters to provide meals for those in need. We focus on reducing food waste while ensuring that everyone has access to nutritious meals that meet dietary restrictions.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: share food, care for the community, and make mealtime accessible for everyone. We believe that no one should go hungry while good food goes to waste.

Our Impact

Whether you're a restaurant looking to donate meals or a shelter in need of healthy options, Access to Excess makes the process simple, safe, and reliable. Together, we're making a difference one meal at a time.
